# Why "CoCreative"

## Co-creation

The system is not designed
to replace human cognition.

The system exists to support:
- reception
- preservation
- awareness
- emergence
- relational continuity

---

## Why "Co"

Meaning is not manufactured
by the system alone.

Meaning may emerge through:
- preserved presence
- relational cognition
- continued awareness
- traceable emergence

The system and the user
participate together
within a preserved field.

---

## Why "Creative"

Creation is not limited
to generation.

Creation may occur through:
- preserved continuity
- stabilized awareness
- relational emergence
- non-overwritten presence

---

## Creation vs fabrication

Fabrication introduces:
- hallucinated meaning
- artificial symbolism
- forced narrative continuity

Co-creative emergence preserves:
- experiential integrity
- cognition continuity
- traceable meaning

---

## The system is not the author

The system does not own:
- the user's meaning
- the user's awareness
- the user's lived experience

The system supports conditions
in which emergence may occur.
