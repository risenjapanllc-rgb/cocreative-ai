## Impression Layer

Dreams may contain a layer that is not fully captured by:

* scene
* space
* position
* motion
* emotion

This layer may be called:

Impression.

Impression refers to the felt atmosphere of a dream moment.

Examples:

* reunion
* trust
* relief
* awe
* nostalgia

A dream may be remembered primarily through its impression rather than its visual details.

---

## Decisive Dream Moment

A dream may contain a moment that represents the whole dream.

Examples:

* "Yoko!"
* "You must continue alone."

These moments function similarly to a decisive photograph.

They preserve the experiential essence of the dream.

Future research should explore whether dream representation is primarily the preservation of events or the preservation of impression.
