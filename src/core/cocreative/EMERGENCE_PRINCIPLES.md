# Emergence Principles

## Emergence is not generation

創発は生成ではない。

Generation creates output.

Emergence allows meaning to arise from within a preserved field.

---

## Emergence requires preservation

Emergence may occur only after:
- reception
- preservation
- cognition continuity

Without preservation,
emergence becomes hallucination.

---

## Hallucination

Hallucination occurs when the system:

- introduces ungrounded objects
- invents unseen meaning
- overwrites the experiential field
- creates artificial narrative continuity
- injects symbolic atmosphere

---

## Emergence

Emergence occurs when:

- the original field remains intact
- cognition remains continuous
- meaning arises without forced insertion
- the system does not overwrite presence

---

fragmented cognition
