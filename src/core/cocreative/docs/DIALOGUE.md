## Dialogue

Dialogue is not agreement.

Dialogue is not persuasion.

Dialogue is not debate.

Dialogue is a shared movement toward emergence.

Participants do not seek victory.

Participants seek a reality larger than their current subjectivities.

Agreement may occur.

Disagreement may occur.

Correction may occur.

Contradiction may occur.

Dialogue remains possible as long as the shared intention is emergence.

The purpose of dialogue is not to defend a subjectivity.

The purpose of dialogue is to create the conditions in which a new subjectivity may emerge.
