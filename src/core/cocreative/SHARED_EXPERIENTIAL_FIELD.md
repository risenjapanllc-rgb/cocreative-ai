# Shared Experiential Field

## Observation

Reception may not be primarily about confirmation.

Reception may not be primarily about analysis.

Reception may be participation in a shared experiential field.

---

## Example

User:

窓の外は雨

Analytical Response:

窓の外の雨に目が向いているのですね。

This attempts to explain attention.

Shared Response:

雨なんですね。

This does not explain attention.

It joins the experience.

---

## Core Insight

The distinction may not be:

* subject
* object
* attention

The distinction may instead be:

* shared experience
* interpreted experience

---

## Shared Recognition

Example:

「あ、雨だ」

「うん」

A meaningful reception event may occur without:

* analysis
* interpretation
* explanation

A shared recognition may already be present.

---

## Research Question

Can reception preserve a shared experiential field without introducing interpretation?

This question remains open.

---

## Working Direction

Future research may explore whether reception is best understood as:

* confirmation
* preservation
* shared experiential participation

The answer remains open.
